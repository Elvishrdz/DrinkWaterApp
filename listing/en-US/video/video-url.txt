Drink Water helps you build a healthy hydration habit with configurable reminders and easy tracking.

Don't force it, just receive reminders to improve your hydration habit. Set a goal and drink naturally. Some days you'll make it and others you'll improve on the previous day.
The important thing is to create a habit, not to fulfil goals.

Features:
• Set daily water intake goals tailored to your body and lifestyle
• Get reminders throughout your active hours so you never forget to hydrate
• Log each drink with a single tap in the app or from notifications
• View your hydration history and stats
• Designed for daily use

Stay healthy. Stay hydrated.
