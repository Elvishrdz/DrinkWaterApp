1. Introduction

Drink Water App ("we", "our", or "the App") is a hydration tracking application developed and operated as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and what controls you have over it.

By installing or using Drink Water App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide — stored locally on your device

During onboarding and normal use, the App asks you for:

Data Purpose
Name Personalize the in-app experience
Gender Calculate a recommended daily hydration goal
Weight (kg or lbs) Calculate a recommended daily hydration goal
Activity level Calculate a recommended daily hydration goal
Wake time / sleep time Schedule drink reminders within active hours
Reminder frequency Control how often notifications are sent
Unit preference (ml / oz / cups) Display measurements in your preferred format

All of the above is stored exclusively on your device using Android DataStore (encrypted local storage). It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2.2 App Usage Data — stored locally on your device

The App records the following data as you use it:

  • Water intake logs: amount, timestamp, and entry type (manual, from notification, or skipped).
  • Daily hydration goals: target amounts and effective dates.
  • Streaks: best consecutive days meeting your goal and best drinking-day streaks.
  • Drink presets: custom quick-add amounts you create.

This data is stored in a local Room database on your device. It is not synced to any cloud service and is not accessible by us or any third party.

2.3 Anonymous Usage Analytics — optional, opt-out available

With your consent, the App sends anonymous usage events to Mixpanel to help us understand which features are used and improve the App. Specifically:

  • App opened / onboarding started, completed, or abandoned
  • Drink recorded
  • Custom preset created
  • Preset quick-add sheet opened

No personal data, water log content, or identifiable information is included in these events. Each device is assigned a random anonymous ID that is not linked to your name, email, or any other identity.

  • Mixpanel data is processed on servers located in the European Union.
  • You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy & Legal → Usage Tracking.
  • Once opted out, no further events are sent and the anonymous ID is reset.

2.4 Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to automatically collect crash reports when the App unexpectedly stops. These reports contain:

  • Stack traces (code location of the error)
  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Time of crash

Crash reports do not include your personal data or water logs. They are used solely to identify and fix bugs.

2.5 Advertising Data

If you use the free version of the App, Google AdMob serves advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and use them to show relevant ads, consistent with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Purchasing the Remove Ads in-app product permanently disables all ads and associated tracking.

2.6 Purchase Information

In-app purchases are processed through RevenueCat, which validates transactions and manages your entitlement to the ad-free version. RevenueCat receives:

  • Google Play purchase tokens (issued by Google, not by us)
  • Your entitlement status (whether ads are removed)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, water logs, or personal profile data.
