1. Introduction

Drink Water App ("we", "our", or "the App") is a hydration tracking application developed and operated as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and what controls you have over it.

By installing or using Drink Water App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide - stored locally on your device

During onboarding and normal use, the App asks for:

Data Purpose
Privacy policy acceptance Legally acknowledge how the App uses the information you provide
Terms of use acceptance Accept the guidelines and rules for using the App
Usage tracking consent Choose whether to send app feature usage data for analysis to improve the user experience
Unit preference (ml / oz / cups) Display measurements in your preferred format
Daily water goal Set the amount of water you want to drink each day
Wake time / sleep time Schedule drink reminders within active hours
Reminder frequency Control how often notifications are sent
Notification quick-add amount Log a predefined amount of water consumed directly from notifications

All of the above is stored on your device using the device local storage. We do not operate servers that store it. It is never transmitted to us or any third party, except the portion you choose to back up if you enable the optional Cloud Backup feature (see 2.7).

2.2 App Usage Data - stored locally on your device

The App records the following data as you use it:

  • Water intake logs: amount, timestamp, and entry type (manual, from notification, or skipped).
  • Daily hydration goals: target amounts and effective dates.
  • Drink suggestions: custom quick-add amounts you create.

This data is stored locally on your device, unless you enable the optional Cloud Backup feature (see 2.7).

2.3 Anonymous Usage Analytics — optional, opt-out available

With your consent, the App sends anonymous usage events to Mixpanel to help us understand which features are used and improve the App. Specifically:

  • App opened / onboarding started, completed, or abandoned
  • Drink recorded and source of the "drink" action (e.g.: notification or app)
  • Custom preset created
  • Drink suggestions quick-add sheet opened
  • Reminders tab opened, and reminder active-day changes (which days of the week reminders are on/off, and how many)
  • Cloud backup actions: connecting or disconnecting the backup, tapping manual sync or restore, changing the auto-backup schedule, toggling sync-on-save, and when a sync finishes (with its result). These events include an anonymous count of how many of your own devices use the backup — never the file contents or any account identity.

No personal data, water log content, or identifiable information is included in these events. Each device is assigned a random anonymous ID that is not linked to your name, email, or any other identity. The App does not collect your name, email address, or any other identity information from any source.

  • Mixpanel data is processed on servers located in the European Union.
  • You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy & Legal → Usage Tracking.
  • Once opted out, no further events are sent and the anonymous ID is reset.

2.4 Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to automatically collect crash reports when the App unexpectedly stops. These reports contain:

  • Stack traces (code location of the error)
  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Time of crash

Crash reports do not include your personal data or water logs. They are used solely to identify and fix bugs.

2.5 Advertising Data

The free version uses Google AdMob, which may collect device advertising identifiers to show relevant ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Purchasing Remove Ads permanently disables all ads and associated tracking. We ensure no further connections are made to advertising services once the ad-free purchase is completed. If you notice any continued ad-related activity after purchasing, please report this unusual behavior so we can take immediate action.

2.6 Purchase Information

In-app purchases are processed through RevenueCat, which receives Google Play purchase tokens (issued by Google, not by us) and your entitlement status (whether ads are removed). RevenueCat does not receive any other sensitive information. If you notice any unusual activity, we encourage you to report it so we can take appropriate action. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

2.7 Cloud Backup & Sync — optional, off by default (Google Drive)

The App offers an optional cloud backup so you can protect your data and synchronize it across your own devices. It is disabled by default and only activates after you explicitly connect your Google Drive account and authorize access.

When enabled, the App uploads a backup file containing your app data to your Google Drive account:

  • Water intake logs (amount, timestamp, entry type)
  • Daily hydration goals (target amounts and effective dates)
  • Preferences (unit, time format, notification quick-add amount)
  • A random per-device identifier used only to merge data across your own devices

The backup does not include your name, email, payment data, or any advertising or analytics identifiers.

Where your backup is stored: the file is written to your private, hidden application data folder (appDataFolder) in Google Drive — a Drive area reserved for the App that does not appear among your normal Drive files and is not accessible to other apps. The App requests the drive.appdata scope plus your basic Google profile (email and name) solely to show which account is connected.

The backup is stored in your own Google Drive account. We do not operate any server that stores this data, and the developer cannot read your backup. You can disconnect at any time from Settings → Backup, and you can delete the backup file directly from your Google Drive account.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure. Drink Water App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed through Google Drive is used only to provide and improve the backup-and-sync feature you requested. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and is never transferred to others except as needed to provide the feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition. We do not allow humans to read your Google Drive data unless we have your explicit consent, it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), or to comply with applicable law.
